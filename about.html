About The Club

Where:

A4 class (kitchen)

Days:

Tuesdays & Thursdays

Time:

Lunchtime

Insructions:

If you would like to join the club I recommand to come into A4 on Tuesdays or Thursdays at lunchtimes. Please make sure to take a form home to fill out with your family, keeping in mind that there is a Fee of $15 for the ingredients being provided for the cooking/baking.

Information:

Welcome to a club where creativity meets flavor! Here, we explore the world by baking traditional and modern recipes from different cultures. You will develop hands-on kitchen skills, experiment with new ingredients, and prepare yourself for future culinary adventures. THE ULTIMATE BONUS: EVERY MEETING ENDS WITH TASTING YOUR DELIOUSE CREATIONS.
